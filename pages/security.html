Security in FLUX

How your account and data are protected.

The Basics

FLUX never connects to a bank, card or broker, so there is no real money to lose. Here is how your account is kept safe.

Safe passwords

Your password is scrambled before it is saved, so not even FLUX can read it. You are logged out automatically after a day.

Protection from guessing

Too many login attempts in a row are slowed down, which stops automated password guessing.

Private system details

Behind-the-scenes controls are locked to the site owner.

Fair prices

Every practice trade uses the real market price, so balances can't be faked.

Secrets and Logs

Service keys are never stored in the code, and they are hidden from system logs.

Good to Know

FLUX is a personal project without formal audits. Please don't reuse a password you use elsewhere.
