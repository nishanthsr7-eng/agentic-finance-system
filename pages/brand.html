Zero-Knowledge Sovereignty

FLUX is built on a proprietary Zero-Knowledge (ZK) infrastructure that allows for verifiable transactions without compromising individual privacy. This ensures that while the system is highly transparent for auditing, the identity and assets of the user remain exclusively theirs.

Our "Sovereign Proof" technology utilizes advanced cryptographic primitives to enable sub-second settlement times across distributed nodes, bypasssing traditional banking latency.
