Tech Stack

Under the hood, for the curious.

Prediction Pipeline

Daily prices go through 42 causal features with fractional differencing, then an XGBoost direction model trained on triple-barrier labels with purged, embargoed walk-forward cross-validation. Probabilities are isotonic-calibrated, and GARCH-shaped conformal bands give 80% and 90% price ranges that cover what they claim.

An LLM verifier reads recent headlines and can lower or veto each call. Every forecast is stored and graded when it matures, so the track record on the Advisor page is real.
