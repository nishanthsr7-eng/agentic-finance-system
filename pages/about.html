Why FLUX
Exists

Most stock-prediction apps promise accuracy they can't deliver. FLUX shows you the odds honestly and gives you a safe place to practise. Built by Nishanth S.

The Idea

Markets are hard to predict, and anyone claiming 90% accuracy is usually fooling themselves. FLUX takes the opposite approach: it tells you the likely direction, how confident it is, and the range the price will probably stay in.

Day to day, the forecasts are only a little better than a coin flip, and the app says so. Where they shine is the price ranges, which hold up as often as promised, and knowing when a signal is too weak to act on.

Daily forecasts

Every night, FLUX studies the price history of 25+ cryptocurrencies and US stocks and forecasts the next five days.

A second opinion from the news

Recent headlines are checked for every forecast. Ask the Advisor anything about a forecast or your own finances.

Practise, don't gamble

A practice wallet, a spending dashboard and a strategy tester let you learn without risking real money.
