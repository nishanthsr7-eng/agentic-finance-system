Questions. Resolved.

Quick answers about forecasts, accuracy and the demo.

What is FLUX?

An app that forecasts where 25+ cryptocurrencies and US stocks are likely to move over the next five days, and lets you practise on those forecasts with virtual money.

How are the predictions made?

Every night, FLUX studies years of price history for each asset, estimates the chance it goes up or down over five days, and works out a likely price range.

Why is the accuracy only about 53%?

Because nobody can reliably predict daily prices. FLUX gets the direction right a little over half the time and says so openly. Its strength is the price range, which holds about 8 times in 10. Apps claiming 80–90% are usually testing on data they've already seen.

What does the news check do?

It reads recent headlines for each asset. If the news points the other way, the forecast's confidence is lowered, and you can see why.

Is any of this real money or financial advice?

No. All trades and payments are simulated, and nothing here is financial advice.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Tap "Use demo account" on the login page. It's shared with other visitors and refreshed regularly.

Why is the first load sometimes slow?

The app sleeps when nobody is using it. The first visit after a quiet spell can take up to a minute.
