Privacy Policy

A transparent overview of how we protect and respect your personal data.

Your Data, Your Control

Privacy is a fundamental human right. At FLUX, we believe your personal information belongs to you. Our privacy framework is built to ensure you maintain total control over your digital identity.

Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of information necessary to provide our services and ensure account security.

  • Account credentials (encrypted at the edge)
  • Basic contact information provided during registration
  • Transactional data required for financial reporting
  • Device metadata for security and threat detection

How We Protect It

FLUX employs zero-knowledge architecture, meaning that we cannot view your decrypted data. All personal information is encrypted before it ever leaves your device.
